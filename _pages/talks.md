## Teaching Philosophy

My approach to presenting and teaching is grounded in **accessibility and engagement**. I believe that complex ideas become meaningful when they are connected to real-world experiences and presented with clarity and enthusiasm.

I am particularly passionate about:

- **Science communication** – Making research accessible to non-experts
- **Inclusive pedagogy** – Supporting diverse learning needs
- **Mentorship** – Empowering students to develop their own voices
- **Interdisciplinary thinking** – Connecting psychology with broader societal issues
